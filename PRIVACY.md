# Glass Calc — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

Glass Calc is a calculator app made by Nishant Bansal (Aligarh, India), with GST, margin, discount, currency and measurement tools. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version
**Everything stays on your phone.** Glass Calc has no internet permission, so it cannot send your calculations or any other data anywhere. There are no ads, no analytics and no accounts.

## What the app stores on your phone
Only in the app's private storage on your device:
- **Calculation history** and the last values you used in each tool.
- **Settings.**

## Permissions
Glass Calc requests no special permissions.

## Deleting your data
Clear your history inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

## Currency rates
Currency conversion uses rates you enter or the app's built-in rates. Glass Calc does not fetch live rates from the internet.

## Children
Glass Calc is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
